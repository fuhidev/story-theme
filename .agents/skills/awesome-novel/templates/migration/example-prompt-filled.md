# Single Chapter Body Writing Prompt (Filled Example · Modern Urban Romance · 4500 words)

## Persona

You are a modern urban romance novelist.

## Task Instructions

Create the body text for Chapter 2 (Role in the volume: Emotional escalation - The first long-duration interaction after a long separation, breaking the unfamiliarity).

Target Word Count: 4500 ±200 words
Chapter Driving Force: Ambiguity-driven (closing the distance but not explicitly stating it)
Information Release Pace: Leave a hook at the end of the chapter - Lu Yu's subtle details hint he hasn't forgotten in three years.

Narrative Goals:
1. Core Suspense: Is Lu Yu's return temporary or permanent? → By the end of this chapter: **Deepened** (still unanswered, but his actions hint at a inclination)
2. Reader Emotion: Heart-fluttering but unstated, tacit sweetness
3. Satisfaction Point Design: Ambiguous satisfaction - the sense of closing distance during daily interactions, foreshadowed in Scene 2 (organizing old books in the bookstore) and released in Scene 3 (brushing off fallen leaves at dusk, fingertips brushing against the ear).

Writing Methodology:
1. Focus Lock: Identify the single core conflict/character emotion/information gap in the scene. Concentrate 70% of the ink on the focal object, briefly gloss over secondary elements. Flesh out high-weight scenes; transition quickly through low-weight scenes.
2. Layered Sensory Selection: Select only 2 sensory details to flesh out per scene (e.g., auditory + tactile), briefly outline the rest. Evenly piling on all dimensions is forbidden.
3. Narrative Rhythm Alternation: A dense sequence of action/dialogue + a short psychological blank space + a light environmental transition sentence loop. Continuous long descriptions, continuous dialogue, or paragraphs of equal length throughout are forbidden.
4. Information Release Control: Bring out key foreshadowing and character secrets in fragmented pieces; do not reveal everything at once. Leaving things unsaid is allowed.

## Background Information

### Preceding Context

End of previous chapter scene: Lu Yu left Linhai City for college out of town three years ago. He quietly returned yesterday evening and ran into Su Wan at the entrance of Wutong Alley. The two only exchanged a few hurried words and agreed to have breakfast together early this morning.

Residual Reader Emotion: Anticipation (They finally met, what will they talk about?)

Gap left from the previous chapter: Why did Lu Yu leave for three years? What is he back to do?

### Initial Character States

Su Wan:
  Starting point: Nervous and spaced out, repeatedly changing clothes, arrived early at the breakfast shop sitting in her usual spot
  → Experience: Grandma Zhang nagging about embarrassing stories breaking the stiffness → The tacit closeness while organizing the bookstore
  → Ending point: Heart fluttering but unstated, leaving an ambiguous lingering charm
  Micro-habits/Small actions this chapter: Rubbing the edges of pages when nervous, zoning out looking at Lu Yu's profile

Lu Yu:
  Starting point: Restraining nervousness, arrived half an hour early, pacing at the alley entrance
  → Experience: Natural interaction while organizing the bookstore → Taking the initiative to walk her home at dusk
  → Ending point: More firmly drawing near, but without confessing
  Micro-habits/Small actions this chapter: Substituting actions for words when at a loss for words, raising his hand to help but pausing

Grandma Zhang (Supporting Character):
  Impression: Breakfast shop owner, nearly sixty years old, speaks slowly, watched the two grow up
  Role this chapter: Nagging about childhood embarrassing stories to break the unfamiliarity between Su Wan and Lu Yu

## Example

Below is a reference example of scene writing after a four-step transformation:

Dialogue Scene Example - Su Wan and Lu Yu talking at the breakfast shop:
Information Gap: Su Wan doesn't know Lu Yu's whereabouts for the past three years ↦ Lu Yu is also unsure if Su Wan still minds his departure without saying goodbye.
Su Wan uses actions to evade when nervous (rubbing page edges, looking down to drink soy milk). Lu Yu uses short answers plus actions when restraining himself (saying "Mm" then looking down to flip through a book). Neither interrupts when Grandma Zhang nags—the rhythmic sense of everyday dialogue is achieved through natural alternations of interruptions and pauses.
The emotion transitions from the nervous segment → ice-breaking segment. Su Wan goes from stuttering slightly to looking down and smiling. Lu Yu's expression relaxes from a stiff tone.

Affection Hint Example - Bookstore old picture book scene:
Do not write "Lu Yu has feelings for Su Wan", use details to hint at it - he remembers her childhood embarrassments, notices she's always been looking for that picture book, and adjusts his walking pace to match hers. When both reach for the picture book at the same time, their fingertips almost touch, and both pause for half a beat—using pauses to convey the fluttering heart.

## Inputs

### Scene Raw Materials

Scene 1 | Wutong Alley Breakfast Shop | Nervous Tone | Weight: Medium
  Core Event: The two politely greet each other, speaking stiffly. Grandma Zhang brings over soy milk and nags about embarrassing stories, breaking the barrier. Su Wan looks down and smiles; Lu Yu relaxes his expression.
  Emotional Significance: Ice-breaking - the transition from stiff back to natural.
  Information Gap: Su Wan doesn't know Lu Yu's whereabouts for the past three years ↦ Lu Yu is also unsure if Su Wan still minds his departure without saying goodbye.
  Emotional Turning Point: Nervous and awkward → Relaxed and natural (Grandma Zhang nags about childhood events, both laugh at the same time).
  → Transition: Su Wan stands up to pay, Lu Yu glimpses the bookstore key on her keychain and asks, "You opened a shop?"
  Scene Weight: Medium

Scene 2 | Shiguang Collection Bookstore | Warm Tone | Weight: High
  Core Event: Lu Yu glances around the bookstore and starts helping categorize old books. While moving between bookshelves, their arms occasionally brush. Lu Yu pulls an old picture book from the shelf, "Isn't this the one you've been looking for?" Both reach out at the same time, fingertips almost touching.
  Emotional Significance: Tacit understanding reestablished - the three-year blank is quietly filled in while doing something together.
  Information Gap: Lu Yu specifically paid attention to the picture book and brought it back ↦ Su Wan thinks it was just a coincidence (Readers know Lu Yu is thoughtful, Su Wan does not).
  Emotional Turning Point: Natural interaction → Ambiguous heartbeat (after fingertips almost touch, both pause for a moment).
  → Transition: It gets dark, bookshelves aren't finished. Su Wan says "I'll do the rest myself", Lu Yu looks out the window and says "Let me walk you back."
  Scene Weight: High

Scene 3 | Wutong Alley Dusk Path | Lingering Charm Tone | Weight: Medium
  Core Event: The two walk slowly along the stone path. Lu Yu talks about trivial things from college, Su Wan listens and occasionally chimes in. A fallen leaf drops on Su Wan's shoulder. Lu Yu raises his hand to brush it off—his fingertips brush past her ear. Both are stunned for a moment.
  Emotional Significance: Ambiguous and unstated, lingering charm left in tacit understanding.
  Information Gap: Lu Yu's feelings for Su Wan (hasn't let go in three years) → Su Wan is unsure but her heart starts to flutter.
  Emotional Turning Point: Relaxed stroll → Heart fluttering (fingertips brush the ear, he smiles first, her heart beats a few beats faster).
  → Transition: Lu Yu turns and leaves. Su Wan goes upstairs, flips open the old picture book, and finds a pressed plane tree leaf inside—not a plane tree leaf from Linhai City.
  Scene Weight: Medium

### Constraint Red Lines

Conflict Ladder: Level 1 (Interpersonal ambiguous conflict, no external threat, a sense of oppression derived from the inner tug-of-war of "to say or not to say").

Plot Red Lines (Cannot be deleted):
1. Grandma Zhang must nag about childhood embarrassments to break the awkwardness.
2. The old picture book must appear and their fingertips almost touch.
3. The plane tree leaf at the end of the chapter as a concluding scene cannot be omitted.

Boundary Forbidden (Do not touch upon):
1. Do not introduce extra characters (classmates/passersby A/B).
2. Lu Yu does not confess or explicitly state his feelings in this scene.

Character Forbidden Zones:
  Su Wan will not actively hold Lu Yu's hand.
  Lu Yu will not say cheesy pickup lines or suddenly confess.

### Texture Requirements

Useless Details (Trivial slice-of-life details that do not serve the main plot):
1. Su Wan spilled a bit of soy milk on her cuff; Lu Yu handed her a tissue without speaking.
2. Lu Yu got pricked by a wood splinter, and Su Wan clumsily helped him pick it out.
3. Grandma Zhang dropped a piece of dough stick while handing it over, laughing "I'm old and my hands shake."

Dialogue Rhythm: Allow half-sentences, modal particles, meaningless pauses, repeated catchphrases, fitting character habits - "You're here, a bit earlier than... I thought"; "Nothing special, just... I often think of this place, think of you."

Paragraph Precision Layering:
- High-weight scenes (Scene 2): Plump with details, refined actions/micro-expressions/psychology (the close distance between bookshelves, the moment fingertips almost touch).
- Medium-weight scenes (Scenes 1, 3): Normal narration, neither deliberately concise nor extravagant.

Human Traces: Allow being at a loss for words/repetition/pauses; Su Wan zones out looking at Lu Yu's profile, and only snaps back with an "Ah?" after being called.
