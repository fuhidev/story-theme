# Western Fantasy Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable genres: western-fantasy

## High-Frequency AI Flawed Sentences: Good vs. Bad Examples

### 1. Piling up Magic Descriptions

❌ "He chanted a spell, and raging flames ignited in his hands."
✅ "He spread his hands open. His palms tickled a bit, like being bitten by a mosquito. He closed his fingers together and chanted two words. Nothing happened. He chanted it again."

### 2. Formulaic Race Descriptions

❌ "Elves have pointed ears, long silver hair, and a noble and elegant temperament."
✅ "Her ears were pointed. He didn't know when he was a kid and got his ear pulled by her. Now he knew it was born that way, and got used to it."

### 3. Direct Statement of Emotion

❌ "A sense of mission surged in his heart."
✅ "He picked up the sword from the ground. The sword was heavier than expected. He switched hands; the carved patterns on the hilt dug into his hand. He padded it with his sleeve and hoisted it onto his shoulder."

### 4. Parallel Structure Analysis

❌ "Perhaps it was a problem with the dragon, perhaps with the castle, or perhaps with himself."
✅ "He sat on the steps. The steps were cold, his pants were thin, and it dug into him. He wanted to change spots, but was too lazy to move."

### 5. Excessive Scene Descriptions

❌ "The castle stood towering, its spires reaching straight into the clouds."
✅ "There were cracks in the castle walls. When he walked past, he counted them; there were seven. The longest one could fit two fingers inside."

## Writing Key Points

1. **Write magic through failure** — Not "successfully cast a spell," but chanting twice with no reaction, itchy palms, trembling hands.
2. **Write races through daily life** — Pointed ears, long lifespan, magic; use concrete life details to touch upon them.
3. **Write mission through heaviness** — Hoisting a sword, switching hands, padding sleeves; the sense of mission is reflected in action, not inside the heart.
4. **Write fantasy through dilapidation** — Castles have cracks, sword hilts dig into hands, magic works intermittently.
5. **Write culture specifically** — Western fantasy isn't just "the Middle Ages," it's specific foods, clothing, customs.
