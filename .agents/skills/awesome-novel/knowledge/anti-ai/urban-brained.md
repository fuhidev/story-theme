# Urban Brained Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable genres: urban-brained, urban-cultivation, urban-high-martial

## High-Frequency AI Flawed Sentences: Good vs. Bad Examples

### 1. System Notification Sounds

❌ "Ding, the system detects the host has completed the mission and obtains a reward."
✅ "He froze for a moment. A box floated past his eyes, the words still flashing. He blinked, and the box was still there."

### 2. Ability Awakening Descriptions

❌ "A mysterious power surged into his body, his eyes flashing with a strange light."
✅ "He looked down at his hand. He could see the speed of blood flowing in his veins. He clenched his fist tight, then released it."

### 3. Parallel Structure Shock

❌ "Perhaps he saw it wrong, perhaps the system made an error, or perhaps none of this was real."
✅ "He pinched himself. It hurt. Slapped himself in the face. It hurt even more."

### 4. Ability Usage Descriptions

❌ "He channeled the true qi in his body, striking out with a palm, its power astonishing."
✅ "He stretched out his hand. His fingertips were a bit numb, like an electric shock. He pulled his hand back, his palm still burning hot."

### 5. Direct Statement of Emotion

❌ "He felt both surprised and delighted."
✅ "He grinned, wanting to laugh, but felt something was off. The corners of his mouth froze in mid-air."

## Writing Key Points

1. **Write systems/abilities through specific experiences** — Not "obtained an ability," but "seeing blood flow speed," "palms burning hot," "tinnitus."
2. **Abilities have costs** — Golden fingers (cheats) aren't given for free; clearly write out the side effects.
3. **Write shock through actions** — Pinching oneself, slapping oneself, repeatedly checking; not "surprised and delighted."
4. **Keep daily life grounded** — Even ability users have to squeeze onto the subway, order takeout, and get scolded by the boss.
5. **Use restraint with explanations** — Don't explain all the settings in one breath; let the readers discover them slowly.
