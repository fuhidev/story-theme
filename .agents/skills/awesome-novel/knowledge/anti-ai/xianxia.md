# Eastern Xianxia Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable genres: xianxia

## High-Frequency AI Flawed Sentences: Good vs. Bad Examples

### 1. Realm Breakthrough Descriptions

❌ "He felt spiritual power surging into his meridians, his dantian heating up, and his realm began to loosen."
✅ "He clenched his fists tight. His nails dug into his flesh. It hurt so much his vision went white, but something seemed to loosen in the seams of his bones."

❌ "Spiritual Qi poured into his head; a breakthrough was right before his eyes."
✅ "He sat cross-legged all night. His knees went numb, losing all sensation. Just before dawn, his stomach suddenly growled."

### 2. Combat Move by Move

❌ "He aimed at the enemy's vital spot, thrusting out his sword, going straight for the throat."
✅ "The tip of the sword trembled. He gritted his teeth and pushed forward, his wrist aching terribly—the opponent dodged sideways."

### 3. Excessive Magical Item Descriptions

❌ "The spiritual sword radiated a blue light, ancient runes carved on its blade."
✅ "The sword hilt dug into his hand. He looked down; the wood was worn with indentations, looking like it had been used for many years."

### 4. Direct Statement of Emotion

❌ "His heart shuddered, suddenly feeling the pressure."
✅ "His foot took a half-step back. His heel hit a rock; there was nowhere else to retreat."

### 5. Parallel Structure Perception

❌ "Perhaps spiritual Qi was insufficient, perhaps his injuries hadn't healed, or perhaps the heavenly tribulation was too fierce."
✅ "He coughed twice. There was a metallic taste of blood in his throat. His hands were shaking too—didn't know if it was from exhaustion or fear."

## Writing Key Points

1. **Cultivation must be arduous** — Don't write about spiritual Qi automatically surging in; write about specific exhaustion, soreness, pain.
2. **Combat must be rough** — Don't write move names; write trembling hands, panting, mistakes.
3. **Use the body for emotions** — Nervous = stepping back, clenching fists, biting lips, not "heart shuddering."
4. **Environment fits the character** — Is the mountain gate cold? Write the specific sensation; don't write "ethereal mist swirling."
5. **Dialogue has a sect flavor** — Cultivators shouldn't talk too formally; use "we," "I (rough)," or "poor daoist," "this humble one" to distinguish status.
