# Suspense / Paranormal Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable genres: suspense-paranormal, suspense-brained

## High-Frequency AI Flawed Sentences: Good vs. Bad Examples

### 1. Piling up Paranormal Atmosphere

❌ "A gloomy aura permeated the air, making one's hair stand on end."
✅ "The hallway light flickered again. He looked up; the lightbulb was covered in dust, swaying for a long time before stopping on its own. He lowered his head and kept walking."

### 2. Direct Statement of Horror

❌ "He felt a wave of fear, a chill running down his spine."
✅ "He shrank his hands into his sleeves. The sleeves were cold, but he hadn't gone outside. It was colder indoors than outside."

❌ "It was as if something was watching him in the dark."
✅ "He squatted down, pretending to tie his shoelaces. Looked up out of the corner of his eye. Nothing was there. He stood up and left."

### 3. Parallel Structure Perception

❌ "Perhaps it was the sound of the wind, perhaps it was a hallucination, or perhaps it was nothing at all."
✅ "He stood there for a while. The wind blew the curtains up, then they fell back down. He waited a few seconds, and the curtains moved again. He closed the window."

### 4. Supernatural Descriptions

❌ "A mysterious force pushed the door open."
✅ "He looked at the door. The door slowly opened. He took a step back. The door closed a bit again. He waited."

### 5. Direct Statement of Emotion

❌ "His heart rate sped up, realizing something was wrong."
✅ "He swallowed his saliva. His throat was tight, couldn't swallow it down. He shifted his feet on the ground, finding an angle to run."

## Writing Key Points

1. **Write paranormal through daily details** — Lightbulbs flickering, curtains moving, doors creaking; use everyday objects to carry the horror.
2. **Write fear through the body** — Tight throat, shrinking hands into sleeves, finding escape angles, not "hair standing on end."
3. **Waiting is scarier than seeing** — Don't write "saw something," write "waiting, not knowing what it is."
4. **Sound is more important than visual images** — The most terrifying thing in paranormal isn't seeing, but hearing sounds coming from nowhere.
5. **Leave blanks at the end** — Don't explain what it is; leave an image for the readers to be scared by themselves.
