# Sci-Fi / Apocalyptic Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable genres: scifi-apocalypse, war-god

## High-Frequency AI Flawed Sentences: Good vs. Bad Examples

### 1. Piling up Apocalyptic Atmosphere

❌ "The sky was gray, and the air was filled with the breath of death."
✅ "He lifted the tent flap. The smog wrapped around the sun, making it look neither yellow nor gray. His throat tightened, dry and itchy."

### 2. Resource Scarcity Descriptions

❌ "Supplies were extremely scarce, and people lived in deep waters and scorching fire (extreme misery)."
✅ "There were three pieces of compressed biscuit left. He stuffed them back into the tin box, closed the lid, and hid it in the corner under the bed."

### 3. Direct Statement of Emotion

❌ "He felt fear and despair."
✅ "He shrank his hands into his sleeves. His fingers were icy cold; clenching them didn't help. There was movement in the distance; he didn't move, holding his breath to listen."

❌ "Hope ignited in her heart."
✅ "She wiped the dust off the seed bag. The paper bag was old, but the words were still legible. She tucked the bag into her shirt, pressing it against her skin."

### 4. Parallel Structure Analysis

❌ "Perhaps the zombie horde had arrived early, perhaps the signal tower was destroyed, or perhaps some accident had occurred."
✅ "He glanced at his watch. The hands weren't moving. He changed the battery last night, and it stopped in the afternoon. He took the watch off and stuffed it into his pocket."

### 5. Technology Descriptions

❌ "He turned on the high-tech terminal, and a holographic projection emerged."
✅ "There was a crack on the screen. He pressed it with the back of his hand; it lit up, but the words were blurry. He leaned in closer."

## Writing Key Points

1. **Write apocalypse through specific scarcities** — Not "supplies were scarce," but "three biscuits left" or "the canteen hit bottom."
2. **Write hope/despair through actions** — Hiding things, tucking clothes, holding breath.
3. **Write technology through wear and tear** — Equipment is old, broken, needing repair, not brand new.
4. **Write danger through sound** — In the apocalypse, sound is more important than visual images.
5. **Keep the pace oppressive** — It can be slow, so slow it makes people breathless.
