# Urban General Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable genres: urban

## High-Frequency AI Flawed Sentences: Good vs. Bad Examples

### 1. Time Descriptions

❌ "A new day began, sunlight spilling in through the window."
✅ "The alarm rang. Six-thirty. He turned it off, lay there for another three minutes, and the phone rang again."

### 2. Piling up Scenes

❌ "Tall buildings stood in great numbers, heavy traffic filled the streets, the modernized city was bustling and prosperous."
✅ "The intersection was jammed. A ninety-second red light; he counted down a full cycle. The person in front was scrolling on their phone, and in the rearview mirror, he could see the driver was scrolling too."

### 3. Direct Statement of Emotion

❌ "He felt that life was not easy."
✅ "He delivered the takeout upstairs. The customer asked him to help take out the trash, and he said okay. In the elevator, he glanced at his phone; there were thirty minutes left to deliver the next order."

### 4. Parallel Structure Analysis

❌ "Perhaps he was too tired from work, perhaps he lacked sleep, or perhaps he needed rest."
✅ "He leaned against the wall. The wall plaster was a bit cold. He patted his pocket; there was a crumpled cigarette, he pinched it, but couldn't bear to light it."

### 5. Excessive Psychological Descriptions

❌ "He fell into deep thought, recalling all sorts of things from the past."
✅ "He sat in the car without moving. The engine was off, still a bit hot. His phone pinged once; he didn't look at it."

## Writing Key Points

1. **Write urban life through traffic jams/queuing/waiting for elevators** — The most familiar daily life of modern people, more real than "tall buildings."
2. **Write emotions through phones** — Modern people leak emotions through phones: scrolling social media, unfinished videos, unreplied messages.
3. **Write time through countdowns** — Takeout has countdowns, meetings have countdowns; life is precise to the second.
4. **Write exhaustion through not wanting to move** — Not "very tired," but "leaning against the wall," "too lazy to move," "can't bear to light a cigarette."
5. **Dialogue must be fragmented** — Urbanites speak in fragments, without head or tail; they communicate more on WeChat than face-to-face.
