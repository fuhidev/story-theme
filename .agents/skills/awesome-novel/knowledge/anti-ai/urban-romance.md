# Urban Romance Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable genres: urban-romance, modern-romance

## High-Frequency AI Flawed Sentences: Good vs. Bad Examples

### 1. Over-designed Ambiguous Actions

❌ "He slowly approached, their breaths intertwining, heartbeat accelerating."
✅ "He shifted forward a bit, resting his elbows on the back of the chair. She lowered her head to play with her phone, catching his shadow in her peripheral vision. The air conditioner hummed."

### 2. Direct Statement of Emotion

❌ "She felt her heart flutter, her cheeks growing slightly hot."
✅ "She pushed the milk tea over, her fingertips touching the back of his hand, and she flinched back."

❌ "His heart fluttered."
✅ "He bent down to tie her shoelaces, his hand pausing for a moment."

### 3. Parallel Structure Psychological Self-Questioning

❌ "Perhaps the lighting was too dim, perhaps she was too nervous, or perhaps he was too direct."
✅ "She frowned, clenching her palms—was she too impulsive just now?"

### 4. Cliche Atmosphere

❌ "The morning sunlight spilled on the windowsill, everything was so beautiful."
✅ "A ray of light peeked in through the gap in the curtains, landing on the back of her hand, a little warm."

### 5. Intentionless Dialogue

❌ "Morning," "Have you eaten," "How's today going"
✅ Delete greeting-style dialogues; use actions/silence to drive the story forward instead.

## Writing Key Points

1. **Ambiguity must be restrained** — Don't design a complete ambiguous process; let the details speak for themselves.
2. **Externalize emotions through actions** — Heart fluttering = chopsticks pausing, fingers flinching back, unsteadiness when turning around.
3. **Keep scenes grounded** — Be specific, down to the milk tea shop's sign, the subway station name, the roadside stall.
4. **Dialogue must have tension** — Every line of dialogue must have an intention (probing/hiding/rejecting/obtaining).

*Other genres (xianxia, suspense, etc.) can be expanded as needed, following the same format.*
