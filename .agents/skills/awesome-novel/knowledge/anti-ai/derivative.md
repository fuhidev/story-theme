# Male-Oriented Derivative Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable genres: derivative, male-derivative

## High-Frequency AI Flawed Sentences: Good vs. Bad Examples

### 1. Counterattack (Comeback) Descriptions

❌ "He didn't expect to have this day, everyone was looking at him."
✅ "He still walked with his head down. His phone rang, a message. He glanced at it, locked the screen, and kept walking."

### 2. Face-Slapping Descriptions

❌ "Everyone was stunned, they didn't expect the trash to actually have this kind of strength."
✅ "He put down the thing in his hand. Nobody around spoke. He waited a bit, still nobody spoke. Later someone coughed, and only then did he walk forward."

### 3. Parallel Structure Shock

❌ "Perhaps he was pretending, perhaps he pulled strings, or perhaps all this was an illusion."
✅ "He pushed up his glasses. Pushed them up again. There was no dust on the lenses. He didn't know where to look."

### 4. Direct Statement of Emotion

❌ "He felt an unprecedented sense of satisfaction."
✅ "He leaned back in his chair and put his feet up on the desk. The water in the cup sloshed but didn't spill. He smiled, making no sound."

### 5. "Acting Cool" (Posturing) Descriptions

❌ "He smiled faintly, hiding his achievements and fame."
✅ "He turned and walked away. Took two steps, put his hands in his pockets. His jacket was a bit long, drooping at the back; he ignored it."

## Writing Key Points

1. **Write counterattacks through daily life** — What do you do after succeeding? Not celebrating, but continuing original habits.
2. **Write face-slapping through silence** — Not "everyone was shocked," but nobody speaking, waiting, waiting two seconds.
3. **Write acting cool through small actions** — Not "smiled faintly," but hands in pockets, jacket drooping down, not bothering to care.
4. **Write satisfaction through relaxation** — Satisfaction isn't laughing out loud; it's putting feet up, rocking the chair, doing nothing.
5. **Keep the pace fast** — Male-oriented derivative pace leans fast; don't drag out face-slapping.
