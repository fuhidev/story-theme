# Anime / Game Derivative Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable genres: anime-derivative, game-sports

## High-Frequency AI Flawed Sentences: Good vs. Bad Examples

### 1. Skill Release Descriptions

❌ "He unleashed his ultimate move, shining with boundlessly radiant light, its power astonishing."
✅ "He pushed the controller forward. The screen flashed, and the character moved. He stared at the health bar, his heart beating wildly."

❌ "A flash of sword light passed, and the enemy was reduced to ashes."
✅ "He swiped the mouse. The character jumped up and slashed down. A sound effect played, and the monster fell. He let out a breath."

### 2. Game State Descriptions

❌ "His character's health was bottoming out, the situation was critical."
✅ "Only a sliver of health left. He moved the mouse to the left, then to the right, hesitating for two seconds. He switched to a different skill."

### 3. Parallel Structure Analysis

❌ "Perhaps the network lagged, perhaps the keyboard was broken, or perhaps he was just unlucky today."
✅ "He tapped the keyboard. No response. Tapped it again. The screen was stuck there, spinning in circles. He flipped the keyboard over and knocked it a few times."

### 4. Direct Statement of Emotion

❌ "He felt victory was in sight."
✅ "He leaned forward a bit. The corner of his mouth turned up slightly, then pressed down again. There was still one wave of minions."

### 5. Overreacting Characters

❌ "He turned pale with fright, not knowing what to do."
✅ "He froze for two seconds. Then he took off his headphones and scratched his head. 'What's this situation?'"

## Writing Key Points

1. **Write gaming from outside the screen** — Controller vibrations, keyboard feel, screen glare are more realistic than describing inside the game.
2. **Write operations with hesitation** — Switch skills or not? Attack or not? Hesitation has more dramatic tension than decisiveness.
3. **Write victory with restraint** — Don't jump up and cheer; it's a twitch of the mouth, a sigh, a breath of relief.
4. **Write failure with small actions** — Smashing the keyboard, drinking water, rubbing eyes, not "being very frustrated."
5. **Dialogue must use memes** — Gamers and anime fans speak with memes, not formal written language.
