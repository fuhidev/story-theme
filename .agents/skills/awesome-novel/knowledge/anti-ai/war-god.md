# War God / Live-in Son-in-law Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable genres: war-god, urban-high-martial

## High-Frequency AI Flawed Sentences: Good vs. Bad Examples

### 1. Identity Contrast Descriptions

❌ "He hid his identity and became a live-in son-in-law for three years, no one knew his true identity."
✅ "He tied his apron. Tied it backward, untied it and did it again. His mother-in-law was urging him again, so he quickened his pace."

### 2. Face-Slapping Preparation

❌ "The corner of his mouth curled into a cold sneer, a plan already in his mind."
✅ "He put out his cigarette. He didn't look at the cherry when extinguishing it, and burned his finger. He shook his hand and flicked the butt into the trash can."

❌ "It's time to let them know the truth."
✅ "He stood at the door without going in. It was quite lively inside. He wiped his hands on his pants, still not having figured out how to bring it up."

### 3. Parallel Structure Mockery

❌ "Perhaps he really was just a piece of trash, perhaps they really misjudged him back then, or perhaps everything was a false impression."
✅ "He stood there; nobody paid attention to him. He wasn't in a hurry either, just rolled up his sleeves. A loose thread stuck out from the sleeve; he tugged at it, but it wouldn't break."

### 4. Direct Statement of Emotion

❌ "His heart was as calm as water."
✅ "He picked up his wine glass and took a sip. The wine was cold. He took another sip. Nobody was looking at him, so he drank as he pleased."

### 5. Identity Revelation

❌ "He slowly raised his head, a glint of sharp light flashing through his eyes."
✅ "He took off his sunglasses. Took them off slowly, eyelids drooping. Looking like he just woke up. Someone nearby shouted, but he didn't hear it."

## Writing Key Points

1. **Write live-in son-in-law life through daily details** — Aprons, urging, tying it backward, more real than "hiding identity."
2. **Write face-slapping through preparatory actions** — Extinguishing cigarettes, rolling up sleeves, wiping hands, not "a cold sneer on the corner of his mouth."
3. **Write calmness through slow actions** — Holding a cup, drinking, nobody looking; calmness is expressed through the rhythm of actions.
4. **Write identity revelation through contrast** — Not "a glint of sharp light," but looking like he just woke up, slowly taking off sunglasses.
5. **Write the "trash" trope through suppression** — Not "enduring humiliation," but drinking as he pleases, nobody paying attention, finding things to do himself.
