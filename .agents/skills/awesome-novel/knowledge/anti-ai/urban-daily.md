# Urban Slice-of-Life (Daily) Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable genres: urban-daily

## High-Frequency AI Flawed Sentences: Good vs. Bad Examples

### 1. Piling up Scenes

❌ "The morning sunlight spilled in through the curtains, a new day had begun."
✅ "The alarm rang. He rolled over and pulled the quilt over his head. Five more minutes of sleep."

### 2. Direct Statement of Psychological Activities

❌ "She felt exceptionally nervous today."
✅ "She clutched her phone in her hand. The screen lit up and went dark, without being tapped."

❌ "He felt somewhat expectant in his heart."
✅ "He dug that shirt out of the wardrobe, shook it out, and then hung it back up."

### 3. Intentionless Dialogue

❌ "Good morning," "Good morning," "How have you been lately," "Is everything alright?"
✅ Dialogue must have an intention to be written: probing, rejecting, fishing for information, covering up.

### 4. Formulaic Emotion Descriptions

❌ "Her eyes grew moist, and tears burst from her eyes."
✅ "She lowered her head and blinked hard. Her eyelashes were wet, sticking together."

❌ "He stood frozen in place, his heart filled with mixed feelings."
✅ "He opened his mouth, then closed it again. The soles of his feet felt like they were filled with lead, unmoving."

### 5. Environment Descriptions Disconnected from Characters

❌ "A faint coffee aroma permeated the cafe, and gentle music flowed in the air."
✅ "The cafe was playing an old song. He didn't know the name. She stirred the ice cubes in her cup, clinking out fine, crisp sounds."

## Writing Key Points

1. **Daily life must be fragmented** — Write real trivialities: alarms, traffic jams, takeout, waiting for elevators.
2. **Use actions for emotions** — Nervous = clutching phone, shaking leg, biting nails; Happy = laughing out loud, talking more.
3. **Dialogue has subtext** — What is said on the surface is different from what is thought inside.
4. **Scenes must be specific** — What color is the milk tea shop's sign? Which subway station? Road name?
