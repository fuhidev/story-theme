# Anti-Japanese War / Espionage Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable genres: anti-japanese-war

## High-Frequency AI Flawed Sentences: Good vs. Bad Examples

### 1. Battlefield Atmosphere

❌ "Amidst the hail of bullets, the soldiers fought bloody battles."
✅ "He lay flat on the ground. His ears buzzed, not knowing if it was artillery or his own blood flowing. Dirt got in his mouth, the grit setting his teeth on edge."

### 2. Intelligence Transmission

❌ "He hid the intelligence in a book and successfully passed it to the organization."
✅ "He folded the paper into a tofu block size and stuffed it under his shoe sole. It chafed when he walked, but he was used to it."

### 3. Direct Statement of Emotion

❌ "The flames of hatred burned in his heart."
✅ "He gripped that photo and looked at it for a long time. There was blood on it, he wiped it with his sleeve, and the sleeve turned red too."

❌ "She felt nervous."
✅ "Her fingers kept trembling. Clutching her hem, clutching for a long time, the hem wrinkled. Later she simply put her hands behind her back."

### 4. Parallel Structure Analysis

❌ "Perhaps the enemy had discovered it, perhaps the intelligence was wrong, or perhaps this was a trap."
✅ "He stood smoking at the street corner. Finished one, lit another. By the third cigarette, someone patted his shoulder."

### 5. Tense Dialogue

❌ "'The situation is urgent, we must act immediately!'"
✅ "'Let's go.' He stood up and crushed the cigarette butt. He didn't look at the person's face."

## Writing Key Points

1. **Write the battlefield through physical sensations** — Tinnitus, the smell of dirt, numb legs, not "a hail of bullets."
2. **Write intelligence transmission through small actions** — Hiding in shoe soles, between book pages, stuffing into wall cracks; specific locations and tactile sensations.
3. **Write tension through repetition** — Smoking, cleaning a gun, fiddling with objects; repetitive actions are more realistic than "nervousness."
4. **Dialogue must be short** — Underground workers' dialogues are all coded; save words whenever possible.
5. **The cost must be specific** — Sacrifice isn't a glorious slogan; it's a photo, an unsent letter.
