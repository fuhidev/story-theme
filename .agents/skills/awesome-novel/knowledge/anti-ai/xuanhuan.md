# Xuanhuan Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable genres: xuanhuan, xuanhuan-brained

## High-Frequency AI Flawed Sentences: Good vs. Bad Examples

### 1. Bloodline / Talent Awakening

❌ "The power of his bloodline awakened, a golden light enveloping his entire body."
✅ "He stared at his hands. The veins on the back of his hands were noticeably thicker than usual, his fingertips a bit hot."

### 2. Trash Comeback

❌ "Everyone didn't expect that this youth deemed as trash actually hid astonishing talent."
✅ "It had been three years of being called trash every day. He already treated the insults like wind past his ears. After the test today, it was still the same old result."

### 3. Pill / Treasure Acquisition

❌ "He obtained an ancient pill, containing astonishing energy."
✅ "That pill was a bit black. He hesitated for a moment, but swallowed it in one gulp anyway. It was so bitter it brought tears to his eyes."

### 4. Parallel Structure Mockery

❌ "Perhaps he really was ruined, perhaps this was fate, or perhaps he shouldn't have stepped into the cultivation world to begin with."
✅ "He listened with his head down. When the scolding was over, he patted the dirt off his pants, stood up, and left."

### 5. Direct Statement of Emotion

❌ "A towering inferno of rage ignited in his heart."
✅ "He clenched his teeth so tight his temples throbbed. His fingers pinched his cuffs, knuckles turning white."

## Writing Key Points

1. **Write talent through physical changes** — Not "awakened," but hot hands, bulging veins, sore eyes.
2. **Write 'trash' through daily details** — What do they do after being bullied? Not a burst of anger, but numbness, habit, or a small physical action.
3. **Write comeback through costs** — Don't write "obtained a divine weapon," write "digs into the hand," "too heavy to lift," "need a few days to get used to it."
4. **Write combat through psychology** — Hesitation, regret, fear are more important than victory.
5. **Write treasures through flaws** — Everything has a price; writing it out makes it real.
