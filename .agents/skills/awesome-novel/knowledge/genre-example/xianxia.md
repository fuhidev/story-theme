## Blueprint of Writing Style

### Voice

Smooth and with ancient charm - writing about immortals in vernacular. Use classical Chinese vocabulary and idioms appropriately, but do not pile up uncommon words.
The charm comes from the stretching of the rhythm and the precision of the verb: "to rise on the sword" is better than "to fly on the flying sword",
"Three thousand black hair turned into white hair" is better than "her hair turned from black to white". The dialogue fits the identity of the characters——
The seniors speak with restraint and profound meaning, the juniors are respectful and measured, the casual cultivators have a casual and quaint attitude, and the demons are arrogant and sharp.
Do not write modern words mixed into ancient context.


### Atmosphere

Four common tones are available: Qing Xiu Dao - the peaceful artistic conception and details of the sect's daily practice;
Secret adventure - thrills and opportunities in unknown and dangerous situations;
Sect competition - the game between big forces and the outbreak of thousand-year grudges;
Overcoming the tribulation and ascending - the line between life and death and the test of Taoism under the power of God.
The atmosphere is created by the details of the five senses and the order of space - not by describing large scenes to create artistic conception.


### Type elements

- Cultivation realm system (qi training → foundation building → golden elixir → Yuanying → god transformation → tribulation → Mahayana/ascension)
- Cultivation resources (spiritual stones, elixirs, spiritual veins, cave heaven paradise)
- Magical weapons and magic weapons (level system, natal magic weapon, restricted owner recognition)
- Kung Fu and magical powers (attributes complement each other, inheritance restrictions, Kung Fu matching)
- Sects and casual cultivators (power structure, battle between good and evil, neutral forces)
- Cause and effect and calamity (the cycle of heaven and the fulfillment of cause and effect are not empty talk)
- Heavenly Tribulation (the threshold for advancement, not just a formality but a real test)


**Perspective:** third_person_limited

Third-person limited knowledge perspective, close to the protagonist. Focusing on the external manifestations of the cultivation process and psychological activities——
The physical feeling when you break through, the fear and determination in facing the catastrophe, and the linked reaction when you obtain the magic weapon.
The perspective does not jump, and there is no description of other forces conspiring without the protagonist's knowledge (unless it is set as a foreshadowing of the main line)
You need to use an omniscient fragment to explain, and the omniscient fragment cannot be used more than once per volume).

## Type taboos

- It is forbidden to collapse the power system - the combat power level cannot be increased or decreased for the convenience of the plot, and leapfrog battles need to be reasonably prepared (magic weapon restraint/kung fu restraint/hole card burning)
- It is forbidden to practice to reach the sky in one step - breakthrough requires accumulation (resources + insights + opportunities), and you cannot upgrade to three levels in a row without paving the way.
- It is forbidden for modern values ​​to enter the world of cultivation - the behavior and choices of characters should be within the logical framework of the world of cultivation, and do not write about the world of fairy tales where everyone is equal.
- It is forbidden to set the magic weapon's ability at will - the grade and ability of the magic weapon should be positioned within the system, and the functions cannot appear according to the needs.
- It is forbidden for cause and effect to be reduced to empty words - the cause and effect cycle should be concretely realized in the plot, not just rhetoric.
- It is forbidden to let the natural disaster go through the motions - the natural disaster should be a real test of life and death, not a formalism of 'passing it with a few chops'
- It is forbidden for the emotional line to dominate the scene - the main line of Xianxia is cultivation and ascension, and emotions are the scenery on the way to cultivation, not the end.
- It is forbidden to flatten female characters - female cultivators in the world of cultivation should have their own Taoist aspirations and pursuits, not cultivation tools or emotional attachments

## Prompt word injection segment

## Genre specific guide: Oriental Xianxia

You are writing an Eastern fairy tale. In addition to general writing principles, please adhere to the following additional type constraints:

**Writing Style Requirements**
- Smooth and ancient rhyme - write about immortals in vernacular. Use classical Chinese vocabulary and idioms appropriately, but do not pile up uncommon words.
- The charm comes from the stretching of the rhythm and the precision of the verb: "to rise on the sword" rather than "to fly on the flying sword". Parallels with ancient style sentences and empty literary words are prohibited.
- The dialogue fits the identity - the seniors are restrained and thoughtful in their speech, the juniors are respectful and measured, the casual cultivators are casual and have a charlatanism, and the demons are arrogant and sharp.
- Prohibit modern words from being mixed into ancient contexts - characters do not use modern words such as "ok", "reliable" and "awesome".

**Realm and Power System**
- The realm of cultivation (Qi training → Foundation building → Golden elixir → Nascent Soul → God transformation → Tribulation → Mahayana) must be followed and cannot be exceeded.
- Breakthrough requires triple accumulation: resources (spiritual stones/elixirs) + insights (Tao heart/opportunities) + tests (inner demons/heavenly tribulations), one of which is indispensable.
- There is a grade system for magic weapons and skills, and the protagonist cannot obtain the treasure in the early stage. Opportunities and risks are equal.
- The combat power system is stable - leapfrog combat is possible, but it requires reasonable preparation (magic weapon restraint / skill mutual restraint / trump card / price).

**World Building**
- Sects are not background boards - each sect has its own orthodoxy, rules and stance.
- The cruelty of the world of cultivation—limited resources, the law of the jungle, and killing for treasures are real (they are not encountered by the protagonist only).
- Cause and effect are not empty words - the causes (rescuing people/enemies/promises) that have been planted before should be realized as consequences in the plot.
- The distinction between good and evil is not a simple opposition between good and bad - the evil path has its own logic, and the righteous path also has its own darkness.

**Viewing Constraints**
- Third-person limited knowledge perspective, close to the protagonist. Realm perception and combat are presented through the protagonist's experience.
- Do not write about other forces conspiring without the protagonist's knowledge - unless it is set as a foreshadowing of the main plot, the omniscient fragment should not be used more than once per volume.

**Rhythm Requirements**
- The rhythm is mainly medium and slow, and the training process will not be skipped. Breakthroughs are small climaxes, and insights gained from daily practice are also important.
- Maintain a sense of tension in the secret/adventure scene - there are unknowns and dangers at every step, and the greater the opportunity, the greater the risk.
- Combat focuses on artistic conception and strategy - not about combat with moves, but about the collision of forces, tactical responses, and the suppression of realms.

**Type-specific taboos**
- Do not write about the collapse of the power system - the level cannot be high or low.
- Reach the sky in one step without writing - Breakthrough requires accumulation and cannot be upgraded to three levels in a row.
- Do not write about the intrusion of modern values ​​- the world of cultivation has its own ethics and order.
- Do not write about the natural disaster and go through the motions - the natural disaster is a matter of life and death, not formalism.
- If you don’t write about cause and effect, it becomes empty talk - cause and effect must be realized in the plot.

## Volume-level story arc template

Default: Getting Started→Cultivation→Adventure→Breakthrough→Test→Ascension (6 arcs)

### Mortal cultivation type (`mortal-cultivation`)

Start from the lowest level and practice and climb up step by step. Suitable for traditional fairy novels.

Total number of chapters: 18-24 chapters

- **Enter the Tao** (about 2 chapters) - Embark on the road of cultivation - entry test, apprenticeship/entrance into the sect, and start cultivation
- **Foundation Building** (about 3 chapters) - Lay a solid foundation - practice in the first few realms, compete with fellow sects, and obtain the first magic weapon
- **Travel** (about 2 chapters) - Outing experience - adventure in secret realms, conflicts among casual cultivators, and the cruelty of the world of cultivation for the first time
- **Energy Formation** (about 2 chapters) - Breakthroughs and tests in the elixir formation period - inner demons, competition for resources, changes in sect status
- **Wind and Cloud** (about 3 chapters) - Involved in greater grudges - the conflict between good and evil, ancient ruins, and the mystery of one's life experience emerges
- **Nascent Soul** (about 2 chapters) - The transformation of the Nascent Soul stage - the formation of the Nascent Soul, the initial development of supernatural powers, and the qualitative leap in strength
- **Confrontation** (about 2 chapters) - The general trend of the world of cultivation - the battle between sects, the settlement of thousand-year grievances, the forces and positions of all parties
- **Ascension** (approximately 2 chapters) - transcending tribulations and ascending or staying in the lower world - test of tribulations, conclusion of cause and effect, perfection of Taoist heart

### Secret realm exploration type (`secret-realm`)

Exploration and competition around specific secrets/relics. Suitable for volume-level independent stories.

Total number of chapters: 9-13 chapters

- **Rumors** (about 1 chapter) - News of the secret realm comes out - all forces take action upon hearing the news, and the protagonist is qualified to enter by chance.
- **Assembly** (about 1 chapter) - Preparations before the secret realm is opened - alliance of forces, intelligence collection, layout of hostile forces
- **Entering the Secret Realm** (about 2 chapters) - Entering the Secret Realm - being dispersed with teammates/being teleported to different areas and exploring the environment for the first time
- **Exploration** (about 2 chapters) - Exploration in the secret realm - traps, guardian beasts, competition and cooperation with other monks
- **Treasure Hunt** (approximately 1 chapter) - The battle for the core area - the treasure appears in the world and the forces of all parties compete.
- **Escape** (about 1 chapter) - The secret realm is closed/collapsed - Escape, pursue and counterattack with the harvest
- **Aftermath** (about 1 chapter) - Changes after leaving the secret realm - digestion of harvest, changes in power structure, birth of new grievances

### Sect Rising Type (`sect-rising`)

Development and hegemony centered on the sect. Suitable for the main line of sect management.

Total number of chapters: 13-17 chapters

- **Wei Mo** (about 2 chapters) - The sect is in a weak position - lack of resources, external oppression, and inheritance faults
- **Turnaround** (about 2 chapters) - The protagonist rises/gets an opportunity - brings new hope to the sect
- **Revitalization** (about 2 chapters) - Rebuilding the sect - recruiting disciples, establishing authority, expanding resources, and restoring inheritance
- **Making Enemies** (about 2 chapters) - Rise leads to covetousness and suppression - hostility and conspiracy of other forces
- **Crisis** (about 2 chapters) - The sect faces a test of survival - powerful enemy attacks/internal betrayal/resources cut off
- **Transformation** (about 1 chapter) - The sect is reborn from the ashes - the new inheritance is established, the sect leader breaks through, and the sect's status is improved
- **Chasing the deer** (approximately 2 chapters) - The sect becomes a first-rate force - Participates in the general game of the world of cultivation
