# Genre Registry

> Displayed to the author during Phase 2 genre selection. Listed flat, 24 types in total.
> Each entry points to an example file under `knowledge/genre-example/` (the `corpus` field), containing style_blueprint, genre_config, genre_taboos, prompt_segment, story_arc_templates.

---

## Urban Series (7 types)

- **Urban Daily** (`urban-daily`) — Urban romance, real-life slice of life, etc. → `urban.md`
- **Urban Brained** (`urban-brained`) — Male-oriented urban imaginative fiction with cheat systems. → `urban.md`
- **Urban Farming** (`urban-farming`) — Rebirth period pieces, workplace business wars, farming/building urban fiction. → `urban.md`
- **Urban High Martial** (`urban-high-martial`) — Alternate urban reality, universal cultivation systems, aura revival, etc. → `urban.md`
- **Urban Cultivation** (`urban-cultivation`) — Urban fiction using cultivation as the power system. → `urban.md`
- **War God Son-in-law** (`war-god`) — Urban-oriented God of War, Soldier King fiction. → `urban.md`

## Historical Series (3 types)

- **Historical Ancient** (`historical-ancient`) — Focusing on farming, court politics, strategy, hegemony, imperial examinations, etc., without cheat systems. → `historical.md`
- **Historical Brained** (`historical-brained`) — Imaginative historical fiction, usually with cheat systems. → `historical.md`
- **Anti-Japanese Espionage** (`anti-japanese-war`) — Male-oriented military war and espionage fiction during the Anti-Japanese War period. → `historical.md`

## Xuanhuan Series (2 types)

- **Traditional Xuanhuan** (`xuanhuan`) — Trash counterattack, strong reincarnations, high-martial worlds, alternate continent fiction. → `xuanhuan.md`
- **Xuanhuan Brained** (`xuanhuan-brained`) — Imaginative Xuanhuan. → `xuanhuan.md`

## Suspense Series (3 types)

- **Suspense Crime** (`suspense-crime`) — Male-oriented detective, suspense, reasoning. → `suspense-crime.md`
- **Suspense Paranormal** (`suspense-paranormal`) — Male-oriented detective, suspense, horror, Feng Shui occultism, etc. → `suspense-crime.md`
- **Suspense Brained** (`suspense-brained`) — Imaginative suspense paranormal, crime-solving adventure, bizarre revivals, thriller games, rule-based weird tales, etc. → `suspense-crime.md`

## Sci-Fi Series (1 type)

- **Sci-Fi Apocalypse** (`scifi-apocalypse`) — Doomsday, zombies, interstellar, mecha, futuristic tech, etc. → `scifi-apocalypse.md`

## Independent Types (5 types)

- **Western Fantasy** (`western-fantasy`) — Fiction set in a Western-style world background, including fantasy elements like magic, aura, and arcane arts. → `western-fantasy.md`
- **Eastern Xianxia** (`xianxia`) — Fiction set in an Eastern-style world background, including Xianxia elements like immortal cultivation and Daoism. → `xianxia.md`
- **Male-oriented Derivative** (`male-derivative`) — Film/TV male-oriented fan fiction. → `derivative.md`
- **Anime Derivative** (`anime-derivative`) — Game, anime, and other ACG-oriented fan fiction. → `derivative.md`
- **Game & Sports** (`game-sports`) — Online games, e-sports, physical sports, transmigrating into games, or world gamification. → `derivative.md`
