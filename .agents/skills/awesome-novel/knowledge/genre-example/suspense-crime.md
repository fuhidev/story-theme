## Type taboos

- Foreshadowing of direct revelations is prohibited - at most one clue fragment will be given in each chapter, but the core mystery will be revealed too early
- No cheating in investigations - the protagonist's reasoning must have a traceable process, and the reasoning steps cannot be skipped in a flash of inspiration.
- No bloody love lines are allowed - love lines can only be hidden lines, do not overwhelm the guest and take over the host, and do not use emotions to influence your intelligence.
- It is forbidden to reveal the plot from God's point of view - the reader can only know what the protagonist knows, and the case cannot be directly explained by the narrator
- It is forbidden to use light-hearted funny plots to destroy the suspenseful atmosphere - black humor is allowed, but slapstick comedy is not allowed
- Prohibition of stereotypes of villains - opponents should have their own logic and motivations, not just bad guys
- Excessive use of professional terms is prohibited - criminal investigation details serve the plot and characters and do not pile up knowledge

## Genre specific guide: suspense criminal investigation

You are writing a suspense crime novel. In addition to general writing principles, please adhere to the following additional type constraints:

**Writing Style Requirements**
- The writing style is smooth and natural, with a certain degree of tightness and tightness. The narrative has a sense of suspense but does not deliberately suppress the warmth of the text - it maintains the smoothness of reading, and there is no need to break sentences to make the text "hard". The sense of suspense comes from information gaps and foreshadowing, not from a cold narrative tone.
- Colloquial but not casual, in line with the rigor of criminal investigation scenes. Keep the dialogue concise - each line carries information (foreshadowing the character or advancing the plot), and does not write purposeless pleasantries.
- Written and literary narrative tone is prohibited. Instead of writing "The night is like ink" - write "Two street lamps are broken, and only the convenience store sign is reflected in the alley."

**Ambience Requirements**
- Maintain a sense of suspense throughout the whole process, and do not reveal the secret easily. Use environmental details (rain, lights, footsteps, busy phone) rather than direct statements to create tension.
- The sense of tension comes from information asymmetry and unknown threats, not from surprise. Use details to express uneasiness and no exclamation points to express tension.

**Viewing Constraints**
- Third-person limited perspective. Only write what the protagonist sees, hears, and thinks. Do not write scenes where the protagonist is not present. Information that the protagonist does not know is not revealed.
- Perspective jumping is prohibited - jumping between the hearts of multiple characters in the same scene is not allowed.
- Statements focus on the protagonist's direct perception, reducing redundant cognitive verb prefixes - but not absolutely prohibiting them, allowing for natural transitions at key emotional nodes.

**Rhythm Requirements**
- Leave blank space for slow pace. Leave 1-2 periods of reaction time after each clue is advanced to prevent the protagonist from figuring everything out immediately.
- The reasoning process must have traceable steps - see A, associate B, verify C, draw conclusion D. You cannot jump directly from A to D.
- Information release rhythm: Each chapter gives a maximum of 1-2 clue fragments to give readers a sense of puzzle.

**Additional constraints in the supernatural direction (suspense and supernatural)**
- Supernatural elements should have inherent rules - ghosts are not invincible, Feng Shui is not omnipotent, and folk magic has its price.
- The sense of horror comes from the unknown and powerlessness - not relying on sudden scares (jump scare), but relying on the slow accumulation of atmosphere.
- The balance between supernatural settings and realistic logic - you can see ghosts, but the character's reaction should be consistent with the reaction of real people.
- It is forbidden to dehumanize characters for the sake of horror - characters' behavior in supernatural scenes should be driven by survival instincts.

**Additional constraints on the direction of the imagination (suspense imagination)**
- Supernatural rules (strange recovery/strange rules/thriller games) must be clearly established in the early stage, and readers need to know "what the rules of this world are."
- The protagonist relies on observation and reasoning to solve the problem under supernatural rules - not relying on force or universal solutions provided by the system. - Thriller game category: Game rules → Exploration → Danger → Crack → Reward/Punishment, progressive cycle.
- Inconsistencies in the rules are prohibited - once the rules are revealed, subsequent plots must abide by them.
- Imagination allows for stronger protagonist initiative and strategy - but the reasoning process can still be traced.

**Type-specific taboos**
- Do not reveal the secret directly, do not skip the reasoning process, and do not insert emotional drama that has nothing to do with the main plot.
- Do not write lighthearted and funny plots to destroy the suspenseful atmosphere - black humor is allowed, but farce is not allowed.
-Villains are not crazy or purely evil - they have their own logic and motivations.

## Volume-level story arc template

Default: case occurs → preliminary investigation → conflicting clues → key breakthrough → truth revealed → aftermath (6 arcs)

### Unit case type (`unit-case`)

From the occurrence to the detection of a single case, the rhythm is tight. Suitable for first volume or standalone case volume.

Total number of chapters: 6-9 chapters

- **Case Occurrence** (about 1 chapter) - discovery of body or bizarre incident - on-site investigation, preliminary judgment, establishing the basic appearance of the case
- **Preliminary Investigation** (about 2 chapters) - Interview witnesses, investigate suspects, collect physical evidence - each step of reasoning can be traced
- **Conflicting clues** (about 1 chapter) - New evidence overturns the previous hypothesis - the direction is blocked, the alibi is established, and the physical evidence is contradictory
- **Key breakthrough** (about 1 chapter) - The neglected details resurface - change the angle → new discovery → lock the direction
- **Truth Revealed** (approx. 1 chapter) - Confrontation/Capture/Interrogation - Motive revealed, but one detail may be left unresolved
- **Aftermath** (about 1 chapter) - the impact of the case on the protagonist - paving the way for the next case or the main plot

### Serial case type (`serial-case`)

Multiple related cases gradually unfold, suitable for medium to long novels.

Total number of chapters: 10-14 chapters

- **Case 1** (about 1 chapter) - A seemingly independent case is involved, but the protagonist has not yet noticed the connection.
- **Pattern Emerges** (approx. 2 chapters) - Second occurrence - Similarities cause alarm, confirm chain
- **In-depth investigation** (about 2 chapters) - combined case investigation - victim correlation analysis, suspect profiling, technical intervention
- **Pursuit thwarted** (approximately 1 chapter) - Alert the enemy/Fake clues/Internal interference - The investigation is in trouble
- **Key Reversal** (about 1 chapter) - New findings overturn the original profile - Direction adjustment
- **Closer to the Truth** (approximately 1 chapter) – Suspect identified – but one link in the chain of evidence is still missing
- **Closing the net** (about 1 chapter) - setting up a trap/arresting/interrogating/connecting the whole case
- **Aftermath of the Ending** (about 1 chapter) - The case is closed but the shadow remains - foreshadowing of the main plot or changes in character relationships

### Cold case restart type (`cold-case`)

Old cases are newly investigated, and the narrative is told in dual timelines.

Total number of chapters: 8-11 chapters

- **Opportunity for restart** (about 1 chapter) - New clues/old people appear - The dusty files are reopened
- **Review of the Year** (about 2 chapters) - Restore the course of the case through investigation (interspersed with flashbacks)
- **A new look at old evidence** (about 2 chapters) - Use new technology/new perspective to re-examine old evidence - Discover things that were ignored back then
- **Relationship Breakthrough** (about 1 chapter) - The people involved in the case came into contact again - some concealed it, some were afraid, and some planned it
- **The truth of the year** (about 1 chapter) - Restore the full picture of the crime - who was covering up what?
- **Accountability in this world** (about 1 chapter) - justice across time - but the price has been paid

### Paranormal investigation type (`paranormal-investigation`)

Investigation of supernatural events, combining Feng Shui ghosts with detective work. Suitable for paranormal directions.

Total number of chapters: 9-12 chapters

- **Visions** (about 1 chapter) - supernatural events occur - haunted/possessed/feng shui changes, someone asks for help or the protagonist is involved
- **Investigation** (about 2 chapters) - On-site investigation - Understand the entire incident, question the parties involved, and look for patterns
- **In-depth** (approximately 1 chapter) - Get in touch with the core of the supernatural - personally experience paranormal phenomena and confirm that they are not man-made
- **Tracing the source** (about 2 chapters) - Looking for the source of the supernatural - historical grudges/feng shui pattern/someone's obsession
- **Coping** (about 1 chapter) - Solution exploration - exorcism/transcendence/breaking Feng Shui/negotiation
- **Closing** (approximately 1 chapter) - incident resolved - the supernatural subsides, but may leave unresolved hidden dangers
- **Aftermath** (about 1 chapter) - The impact of the event on the protagonist - consumption of spiritual energy/changes in perception of the world

### Rule-bizarre type (`rule-bizarre`)

Weird revival, strange rules, and thriller game. Suitable for the direction of imagination.

Total number of chapters: 8-11 chapters

- **Mutation** (approximately 1 chapter) - rules come/strange events are triggered - the protagonist is forced into a supernatural scene
- **Exploring the Rules** (approximately 2 chapters) - Observation and probing - understanding the rules and taboos of the current scenario through trial and error
- **Survival** (about 2 chapters) - Survive under the rules - hide/cope/exploit loopholes in the rules
- **Crack** (approx. 1 chapter) - Find the way to pass - reasoning + verification + execution
- **Complete/Escape** (about 1 chapter) - Escape from the abnormal scene - but not necessarily win, it may be a tragic victory
- **New Normal** (about 1 chapter) - changes in the world after clearing the level - discovering that a larger and strange rule system is running
