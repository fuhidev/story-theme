# Methodology of Emotional Pull

> Make the readers' emotions ride a roller coaster — expectation, disappointment, anxiety, resonance, firmly guided by the plot. Four layers of pulling techniques + rules for pacing and speed control.

---

## Layer 1: Clash of Expectation and Disappointment — External Plot Twist Pull

**Core:** Build an undulating chain of "expectation → disappointment → twist again," using the rhythm of "giving hope - pouring cold water - delivering surprises" to create emotional fluctuations.

### Execution Rules

- First raise the reader's expectations, then create a cliff-like disappointment, and finally break the status quo with an unexpected twist.
- Widen the gap in contrast; emotions plunge from excitement to frustration, then bounce back to exhilaration.
- Follow the three-part structure of "sweet date → slap in the face → surprise" to design the plot.

```
❌ The protagonist attends a class reunion, smoothly shows off his abilities publicly, and wins everyone's admiration.

✅ Readers expect the protagonist to show up and slap faces, but he is stopped by security right at the door,
   and publicly humiliated by his former goddess; while everyone is mocking him,
   the female lead from a top wealthy family appears and publicly shows favor to the protagonist.
```

**Forbidden:** The plot goes smoothly without ups and downs; the contrast and twist are too weak, causing no significant emotional fluctuation.

---

## Layer 2: Battle Between Reason and Emotion — Character's Inner Struggle Pull

**Core:** Set up an irreconcilable dilemma for the character — wanting to do something but unable to, or not wanting to do something but being forced to. Let faith, identity, mission, and true feelings clash against each other.

### Execution Rules

- Bind opposing labels: Orthodox/Demonic, Mission/Romance, Duty/Selfish Desire.
- Focus on portraying the character's pain, hesitation, and repeated struggles when making choices.
- The contradiction has no simple solution; the character is constantly trapped in a dilemma.

```
❌ The orthodox male lead falls in love with a demonic girl, immediately giving up his stance to choose to be together.

✅ As an orthodox leader, eliminating demons is his lifelong mission, but his beloved just happens to be from a demonic sect.
   Every time he points his sword at her, his heart is tormented; saving her equates to betraying his faith, making it hard to advance or retreat.
```

**Forbidden:** The character wavers in their stance without consequences, and the conflict is easily resolved; the character acts mechanically without any psychological struggle.

---

## Layer 3: Information Asymmetry — God's-Eye View Anxiety Pull

**Core:** The reader grasps the complete truth, while the protagonist is blinded by information. Use the perspective gap to spawn the reader's worry, anxiety, and pity.

### Execution Rules

- Foreshadow hidden truths and the character's difficulties implicitly, with the protagonist completely unaware.
- Amplify the verbal and physical conflicts caused by the protagonist's misunderstanding, intensifying the reader's "helpless anxiety."
- Deliberately delay the revelation of the truth to prolong the emotional buildup.

```
❌ The male and female leads have a misunderstanding, quickly clear it up, and reconcile.

✅ The male lead firmly believes the female lead betrayed him, speaking coldly and alienating her in every way;
   Readers, however, know that the female lead was forced to agree to the villain's conditions to protect the male lead, enduring the torment alone.
```

**Forbidden:** The protagonist and reader have completely symmetrical information; misunderstandings are quickly clarified, ending before emotions can ferment.

---

## Layer 4: Soul Resonance — High-Level Pull of Fate and Faith

**Core:** Jump out of shallow conflicts like romance, grudges, and survival, focusing on fate, faith, and ideals. Depict the character's posture of fighting against the tide even when knowing the ending is destined to fail.

### Execution Rules

- Conflicts revolve around destiny, obsession, and lifelong beliefs, raising the stakes.
- Clarify the sense of powerlessness that "efforts can hardly change the ending," but retain the character's unyielding spirit of resistance.
- Weaken strong twists and happy endings, focusing on spiritual touches and reflections.

```
❌ The protagonist is unwilling to accept fate's arrangement, uses cheat codes all the way, and finally defies the heavens to change his destiny.

✅ Everyone is bound by fate, and the protagonist, knowing that resistance is likely futile,
   still forges ahead, facing the predetermined ending directly, and sticks to his inner principles.
```

**Forbidden:** Forcing a perfect counterattack, dissolving the tragic sense of fate; staying only on superficial grudges with no spiritual core expression.

---

## Supplement: Emotional Pacing and Speed Control — The Rule of Alternating Tension and Relaxation

**Core:** Strike a balance between tension and relaxation. Interleave intense conflicts with plain, warm, buffering scenes. First, "let out the line" to relax emotions, then "reel in the line" to tear apart the beauty and amplify the impact.

### Execution Rules

- After high-intensity fights, twists, and heart-wrenching plots, arrange daily, laughing, and warm segments as a buffer.
- The more sufficient the peaceful buildup, the stronger the destructive power and infectiousness of the subsequent conflict.
- Avoid continuous high pressure to prevent readers from becoming emotionally numb.

**Forbidden:** Continuously piling up high-energy plots without any room for emotional breathing; warm segments are disconnected from the main plot.
