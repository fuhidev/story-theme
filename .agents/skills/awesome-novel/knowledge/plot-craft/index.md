## 1. Value Dislocation Conflict — High-Level Core Conflict

**Core:** Jump out of simple enemy vs friend grudges, and shape clashes of values where both sides' stances are self-consistent and their bottom lines cannot be compromised. There are no absolute good guys or bad guys.

### Execution Details

- Establish firm and contradictory codes of conduct, beliefs, and goals for the protagonist and the opponent separately.
- What both sides do stems from their own stances, not doing evil for the sake of evil.
- The conflict lands on a "dilemma of choice," not simply determining victory or defeat.

```
❌ The villain is jealous of the protagonist, constantly making things difficult and deliberately harming him.

✅ One side holds fast to humanity and individual dignity, preferring death to abandoning moral bottom lines;
   The other side takes the survival of the race as the highest goal, willing to sacrifice individuals, forsaking emotions and rules.
   Their goals clash, making a fight inevitable.
```

**Forbidden:** Conflict stops at superficial physical confrontations like cursing and fighting; shaping purely stereotyped villains.

## 2. Extreme Incremental Environmental Pressure — External Pressure + Countdown

**Core:** Overlay harsh external environments like closure, danger, and resource depletion, introduce hard constraints like countdowns, disasters, and ability degradation, constantly compressing the character's room for action, forcing the conflict to intensify.

### Execution Details

- Attach fatal external crises to the conflict scene: hypoxia, fire, flood, entrapment in dangerous situations.
- Add a clear countdown, continuously shortening the deadline, intensifying urgency.
- External pressure gradually worsens as the plot progresses, rather than maintaining an unchanging environmental state.

```
❌ The protagonist slowly gathers evidence to prove his innocence.

✅ The oxygen in the locked room is only enough for half an hour, while the execution of a loved one is only three hours away;
   The protagonist must find the evidence within an extreme time limit and a harsh environment.
```

**Forbidden:** Relaxed and unrestricted environments, with no time or survival constraints; the environmental state remains completely unchanged throughout.

## 3. Goal Replacement Method — Changing "Seeking Gain" to "Preventing Loss"

**Core:** Replace the protagonist's active, positive goal of chasing benefits with a passive survival goal of avoiding devastating loss. Use "the cost of losing" to amplify the driving force for action and the reader's sense of urgency.

### Execution Details

- Weaken positive temptations like "getting rewards, gaining honors."
- Bind severe consequences: failure means loved ones in danger, family annihilation, end of life, or a complete failure of a lifelong obsession.
- Force the character to move forward, rather than voluntarily pursuing steadily.

```
❌ The protagonist participates in a martial arts tournament just to win the championship and become famous.

✅ Losing the tournament means halting a loved one's medical treatment and the entire family facing a purge.
   Winning the championship is no longer a pursuit, but the only way to survive.
```

**Forbidden:** The conflict's driving force relies solely on shallow positive desires like fame or interest; failure has no real severe consequences.

## 4. Conflict Chain Reaction — Domino-Style Progression

**Core:** A single conflict does not end on the spot; let a small incident trigger a chain of events, spreading from personal contradictions step-by-step to groups, regions, factions, and even worldview-level crises.

### Execution Details

- The initial small conflict acts as the first "domino," spawning new contradictions and events.
- The scope of the conflict expands progressively: Individual → Family → City → Faction → The Entire Worldview.
- The character is swept up by the situation and cannot withdraw midway.

```
❌ A passerby mocks the protagonist, the protagonist strikes back, and the incident ends there.

✅ An ordinary theft consecutively leads to family misfortunes, underground gatherings, and official investigations,
   ultimately uncovering a massive conspiracy involving the manifestation of an evil god.
```

**Forbidden:** Resolving things case by case, where a conflict erupts at a single point and immediately subsides; the impact of the event is always confined between two people.
