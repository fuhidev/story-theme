# Opening Hooks Methodology

> Targeting readers' short-term patience, five universal top-tier opening hooks suitable for all web novel genres.
>
> **Special Constraints for the First Chapter:** Under the platform's traffic logic, the first 500 words determine initial retention. Readers only give 3 seconds to judge, so the first 500 words must accomplish "throwing a stone → splashing water" — stone = conflict/cheat/twist hook, water = opposing visuals, bizarre contrast, emotional impact.
>
> The Three Rejections in the Beginning: Reject slow buildup (start directly with conflict), reject static scenery description (people and events in the first paragraph), reject self-introductions (place world-building later). Applicable to the design of the first chapter of the first volume.

---

## 1. Suspenseful Blank Hook — Reveal the Phenomenon, Hide the Reason

**Core:** Display bizarre and abnormal surface phenomena, completely hiding the reasons. Use informational blanks to arouse the reader's desire to explore.

### Execution Details

- Directly throw out abnormal, counter-intuitive scenes and behaviors at the beginning.
- Only show the results, never explain the reasons — explanation is the natural enemy of suspense.
- Keep the writing extremely concise; no background buildup, no character introductions, no backstory explanations.
- Create multiple linked questions to lock in the reader's attention.

```
❌ My grandfather had a very strange personality, and everyone in the village feared him for a hidden reason.

✅ Every day, my grandfather would light incense for himself and gnaw on candles in front of his own memorial tablet.
   The villagers had always feared him; only later did I learn that the person they truly feared was always me.
```

**Forbidden:** Explaining the reason for the phenomenon at the beginning; large paragraphs building up background and character settings; revealing the whole secret.

---

## 2. Extreme Contrast Hook — Cognitive Dislocation, Absurd Conflicts Break Inherent Cognition

**Core:** The ultimate dislocation of identity and behavior — noble identity + absurd behavior, or powerful character + outrageous daily life, creating a sense of absurdity.

### Execution Details

- Directly throw out a contrasting scene in the first sentence; no buildup, no transition.
- Deliberately create logical dissonance to generate a large number of questions in the reader's mind.

```
❌ Today was Su Jie's wedding day, yet his mood was exceptionally complicated, without a hint of joy.

✅ Today was Su Jie's wedding day, yet he sat quietly in a coffin, slowly eating the offerings on the altar.

✅ The top professional exorcist in the industry received the most outrageous order today —
   to perform an exorcism on the neighbor's husky, just because the dog was fluently reciting elementary math tables.
```

**Forbidden:** Identity matches behavior without contrasting dislocation; the scene is bland and conventional, fitting public inherent cognition.

---

## 3. Front-Loaded Conflict Hook — Stormy Start, Direct Cut into Climax

**Core:** Skip all buildups and throw the protagonist directly into the center of a life-and-death crisis, a desperate betrayal, or an opposing conflict right at the start.

### Execution Details

- Climax right at the start; directly write the results of the conflict, fatal crises, or major betrayals.
- Comes with a triple tension: the oppressive sense of danger, cognitive contrast, and unsolved questions.
- Never slowly build up from causes, daily life, or background.

```
❌ Lu Ming had worked diligently for the sect for three years, frequently targeted by fellow disciples, and slowly realized the sect did not treat him well.

✅ A cold sword tip was pressed tightly against his throat, and Lu Ming suddenly realized:
   Everyone in the sect he had desperately protected for three years wanted to take his life.
```

**Forbidden:** Building up the plot gradually; writing daily life, cultivation, or interpersonal relationships at the beginning without immediate conflict.

---

## 4. Subverted Setting Hook — Worldview Bomb, Defamiliarized Bizarre Start

**Core:** Directly throw out novel worldview rules at the beginning, describing extremely absurd rules in a daily tone to catch the reader's curiosity for the bizarre.

### Execution Details

- Throw out the core subverted setting in the first sentence; do not hide, drag, or put it later.
- Adopt the defamiliarization writing style of "daily tone + abnormal rules."
- Pair with concrete visuals (values, comparisons, behaviors) to ground the setting.

```
❌ The rules of this world are very special; lifespan can be traded. The rich live long, while the poor live short lives.

✅ In this world, there has never been wealth or poverty in money, only the nobility and lowliness of lifespan.
   Chen Ping looked at the 24-hour countdown left on his wrist,
   then at the rich man's panel showing a 500-year lifespan balance, resolution forming in his eyes — he was going to rob time for once.
```

**Forbidden:** Conventional realistic logic at the beginning with no novel settings; hiding the core worldview in subsequent chapters, making the beginning bland and unhighlighted.

---

## 5. Extreme Emotion Hook — Maximum Injustice, Low-Point Accumulation for Rebound

**Core:** Shape extreme injustice and grievances to max out the reader's emotions of anger, frustration, and unwillingness. Use emotional disparity to build up the satisfaction of the subsequent turnaround.

### Execution Details

- Construct a strong contrast of "extreme sacrifice + extreme ingratitude."
- Focus on unfair treatment rather than simple suffering to strengthen the reader's immersive anger.
- The lower the emotions are suppressed, the stronger the explosive satisfaction of the subsequent turnaround.

```
❌ Su Jie guarded the border for many years and suffered greatly. After returning, he endured many grievances and was full of unwillingness.

✅ Su Jie spent ten years guarding the border, bathing in blood to protect his country. On the day he returned home,
   the court enemies he desperately protected sat in the high halls, his wife raised a glass to toast them,
   and his white-haired old father knelt in the blizzard, bowing his head in apology to everyone.
```

**Forbidden:** Simply piling up suffering without opposing injustice; emotions are flat, lacking contrast, and unable to trigger reader empathy.
