# Environment Description: Suspense & Crime

> Overwrites parts of the universal environment description methodology (`environment/universal.md`) that are not suitable for the suspense and crime genre.

- The environment is a carrier of clues. Every detail in the scene could become physical evidence.
- Environmental descriptions are not just for atmosphere; they are burying evidence for the reader. Writing "there are three cigarette butts in the ashtray" might be a clue; it cannot be written casually.
- **Pitfall Avoidance**: Do not write about the environment just for the sake of atmosphere—every sentence of environmental description in suspense/crime must pass the test of "is this a clue or pure atmosphere".
