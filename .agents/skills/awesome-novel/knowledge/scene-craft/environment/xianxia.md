# Environment Description: Eastern Xianxia

> Overwrites parts of the universal environment description methodology (`environment/universal.md`) that are not suitable for the Xianxia genre.

- The environment reflects cultivation realms and the spiritual energy of heaven and earth. Places rich in spiritual energy and places where spiritual energy is depleted are written differently.
- The environment is not just physical; there is also perception on the level of "spiritual sense"—experts use divine sense to perceive the environment, seeing not just vegetation but also spiritual veins and traces of formations.
- **Pitfall Avoidance**: Do not write long paragraphs of environmental description just to show off writing skills—every paragraph of environmental description should serve the character's current actions or state of mind.
