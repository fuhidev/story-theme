# Combat/Confrontation Scene Writing (Mortal Tier)

> Applicable to **mortal/realistic tier** combat—fists, cold weapons, street brawls, cold weapon duels. No supernatural powers.
> For supernatural combat like fantasy/Xianxia, refer to `fight/xianxia.md` and `fight/{genre}.md`.
>
> Each technique starts with a `[Tag]`. The prompt-crafter matches tags based on the core event of the scene, selecting only the corresponding techniques to inject into the Output·Writing Guidelines.

## Tag Selection Guide

| Scene Characteristics | Matching Tags | Selection Reason |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Mortal tier heavy weapons / strength type / fierce style | [Verb] [Feedback] | Use precise verbs instead of "hit/kick/punch"; show strength through environmental feedback. |
| Mortal tier light weapons / agility type / assassin style | [Verb] [Senses] | Use "wipe/pick/drill/wind" instead of "dodge/slash"; use multi-sensory details to build tension. |
| Need to show the power/damage of an attack | [Feedback] [Consequence] | Don't boast with adjectives; rely on impact feedback + environmental destruction. |
| Monotonous combat pacing, reader fatigue | [Pacing] [Senses] | Alternate long and short sentences; switch between four camera shots; bombard multiple senses. |
| Mindless slugfest, no strategy | [Game] | Add tactical motives—exploiting flaws / feigning flaws / leveraging momentum. |
| Want to write "fast" (fast blade/sword/movement) | [Verb] [Feedback] | Write speed through results, not "fast as lightning". |
| Want to write "heavy" (heavy punch/hammer/crushing) | [Feedback] [Consequence] | Write weight through bodily feedback + ground shattering. |


## [Verb] Verb Downgrading + Detail Amplification

**Core:** Delete subjective summary words like "agile, heavy punch, powerful", and break down vague actions into specific body parts, precise distances, force feedback, and sound/physical sensations. Use close-ups instead of summaries.

### Execution Details

- **Breaking down movement:** Avoid "dodge, evade"; specify limb movements, offset distances, and relative positions.
- **Breaking down strikes:** Lock onto the point of impact, add intuitive feedback like bone, flesh, and sound.
- **Do not use adjectives to define throughout**; only use objective visual presentation for effects.

### Fierce vs. Agile Verb Table

| Type | ❌ Forbidden | ✅ Mandatory |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Fierce** (Strength, heavy weapons) | Hit, kick, punch, crash | Bombard, cleave, smash, shatter, crush, chisel, slap |
| **Agile** (Speed, movement, short blades) | Dodge, evade, slash, hit | Wipe, pick, drill, wind, sweep, stick, slide, spin |

```
❌ He agilely dodged a punch and knocked the opponent flying with a heavy blow.

✅ The opponent's fist tore through the air straight toward the bridge of his nose. His head jerked three centimeters to the left,
   the fist wind brushing his cheek, stinging his flesh.
   His five fingers suddenly clenched, finger bones popping crisply. His fist smashed into the opponent's chest cavity;
   the sound of bone cracking rang out clearly. The opponent's body crashed into and overturned the wooden table like a kite with a broken string,
   wood splinters flying everywhere.
```

**Forbidden:** Subjective evaluative words like "agile movements, immense power, heavy and powerful momentum".


## [Feedback] Environmental Interaction + Physical Feedback

**Core:** Do not boast about power with words; let the moves interact with the scene. Use the destruction of scenery, airflow changes, and the movement of external objects to indirectly amplify strength and combat intensity.

### Execution Details

- **Area of effect attacks:** Describe shockwaves spreading, ground cracking, vegetation shattering, and rubble flying.
- **Close-quarters grappling:** Use surrounding objects, walls, and the ground as feedback carriers.
- **Linked physical sensations:** Add the intuitive feelings that flying rocks, airflow, and dust bring to the character/bystanders.

### Universal Power Formula

```
Attack action + Target deformation + Environmental shattering + Shockwave/Aftermath
```

```
❌ He slammed his palm on the ground; the immense power knocked everyone back.

✅ A single palm pressed into the ground, and a shockwave exploded outward. All surrounding fallen leaves were crushed to powder,
   the bluestone floor cracked like a spider web, and flying rubble smashed into bystanders' faces, stinging their skin.
```

### Top-Tier Indirect Writing (Writing Fast, Heavy, Sharp)

```
Writing a fast blade: Sheathing the blade, the person walks ten paces, and the falling leaves behind him are simultaneously sliced in half.

Writing a heavy punch: Before the fist arrives, the wind pressure arrives first, pressing a clear fist imprint into the dirt wall.
```

**Forbidden:** Vague, flowery rhetoric like "earth-shattering, swallowing mountains and rivers"; purely describing the move itself with no environmental interaction.


## [Senses] Multi-Sensory Bombardment — Goodbye to Visual-Only Combat

**Core:** Combat is not watching a movie; it is experiencing it firsthand. Switch between at least 2 senses for every engagement.

### Fixed Material Library

| Sense | Usable Elements |
|------|---------|
| **Hearing** | Explosive pop of fist wind, crisp snap of bones, muffled hiss of blade friction, clash of weapons, rapid panting |
| **Smell** | Smell of blood, rust, gunpowder, burnt dust |
| **Touch (Physical)** | Numbness in the purlicue, stinging palms, tearing soreness in muscles, wind pressure making the chest feel stifled, sweat stinging the eyes |

**Rule:** One engagement must cover at least 2 senses; it cannot be solely visual.


## [Pacing] Camera Movement + Line-of-Sight Guidance

**Core:** Imitate film storyboarding, use camera switching to control reading pacing and enhance the sense of presence.

### Four-Shot Switching Method

| Shot | Purpose | How to Write |
|------|------|------|
| **Long Shot** | Set overall atmosphere, environmental changes | Birds startling, weather anomalies, venue layout |
| **Medium Shot** | Character positioning, move clashes, weapon collisions | Two people weaving through the forest, weapons clashing and sparks flying |
| **Close-Up / Macro** | Beads of sweat, looks in eyes, wounds, flaws, weapon sparks | Cold sweat sliding down the temple, gaze locking onto the opponent's throat |
| **POV (Point of View)** | Present attack from the character's perspective, amplify pressure | The long sword constantly enlarging in the pupil |

### Long Sentences Slow, Short Sentences Fast

| Stage | Sentence Type | Effect |
|------|------|------|
| Standoff Buildup | Long sentences | Oppressive buildup, environment, eyes, aura |
| Moment of Clash | Barrage of 3-6 word ultra-short sentences | Heartbeat accelerating, tension |

### Scene Application Template (Forest Fight)

```
Long Shot: The night was heavy, and birds in the forest startled upward.
Medium Shot: Two dark figures wove through the forest, weapons clashing, sparks flying.
Close-Up: Cold sweat slid down his temple, his gaze locked dead on the flaw at the opponent's throat.
POV: The long sword thrust toward his face, its sharp edge constantly enlarging in his pupils.
```

**Forbidden:** A single fixed perspective throughout, no camera switching; writing only the two people's actions throughout with no environment.


## [Game] The Sense of Game in Action Scenes — Goodbye to Mindless Brawling

**Core:** Every move has a purpose; it is not random striking. Good combat = IQ crushing + predictive game + leveraging desperate situations.

### Three Major Tactical Approaches

| Tactic | Description | Effect |
|------|------|------|
| **Exploiting Flaws** | Staring at opponent's center of gravity, old injuries, footing missteps, moments of imbalance | Readers feel the protagonist is smart |
| **Feigning Flaws** | Intentionally leaving an opening, luring the opponent to rush in, then counter-killing | Reversal satisfaction |
| **Leveraging Momentum** | Sand/dust, terrain, slope, obstacles, opponent's personality habits | Combat is not monotonous |

```
❌ The two sides exchanged over a dozen moves without a clear victor.

✅ He stepped back half a pace, his left foot slipping on a piece of gravel—his center of gravity unstable.
   Sure enough, the opponent seized this opportunity and lunged forward.
   But he smiled. His heel was already firmly planted.
```

**Forbidden:** Both sides purely trading blows with raw strength, no thinking, no tactics, no prediction.


## [Consequence] Visualization of Cost

**Core:** Do not write "was injured", write the consequences.

```
❌ He was slashed by a knife and severely injured.

✅ He knew this hand was ruined the moment he used his left hand to catch the blade.
   Blood flowed down his arm to the crook of his elbow. He squeezed his fist—his fingers could still move, but he couldn't exert any force.
```
