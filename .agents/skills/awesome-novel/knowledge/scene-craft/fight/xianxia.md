> For mortal tier combat, refer to `fight/universal.md`—Xianxia-specific realm suppression, magical treasure duels, etc. overwrite those here.

## Realm Grading — Different Tiers Have Different Combat Effects

There is no "universal writing method" for Xianxia combat. Battles in the Qi Condensation and Soul Formation stages are completely different in everything from power sources to perceptual dimensions. Divided into three categories based on the realm relationship of the combatants:

### 1. Cross-Major Realm Combat (High Realm vs. Low Realm)

> Applicable: Qi Condensation vs. Golden Core, Golden Core vs. Nascent Soul, etc. A crushing defeat.

| Dimension | High Realm Side | Low Realm Side |
|------|---------|---------|
| Essence | No need to lift a finger; pressure alone subdues | Can't harm a single hair of the opponent despite giving their all |
| Verb | A casual wave, a sweep of the eyes, a flick of the sleeve | Revealing all trump cards, burning essence blood, self-destructing treasures |
| Feedback | No environmental feedback needed—opponent can't even withstand the aftermath of the attack | Attacks hit the opponent's protective spiritual light without even a ripple |
| Senses | Overlooking, indifferent, no need for divine sense | Divine sense crushed, difficulty breathing, spiritual energy stagnated |
| Pacing | Extremely short—instant kill | Extremely long—the opponent is playing around |
| Game | None—pure crushing | Only chance: opponent underestimates them → use forbidden secret arts to fight for a sliver of survival |
| Cost | None | Foundation completely destroyed, cultivation ruined, body dies and Dao disappears |

```
❌ The two fought for three hundred rounds without a clear victor.

✅ Before he could even see the opponent's movements clearly, a palm print had already appeared on his chest.
   Spiritual energy poured in from the palm print, instantly sealing the meridians in his entire body.
   He couldn't even feel the pain—the gap was too vast.
```

### 2. Combat Within the Same Major Realm (Same Realm, Different Tiers)

> Applicable: Mid Qi Condensation vs. Late Qi Condensation, Early Foundation Establishment vs. Peak Foundation Establishment, etc. Obvious gap but compensable.

| Dimension | Higher Side | Lower Side |
|------|---------|---------|
| Essence | Deeper spiritual energy, purer spells | Rely on treasures/tactics/trump cards to narrow the gap |
| Verb | Spell power is noticeably greater under spiritual energy infusion | Compensate with more clever sword arts and trickier angles |
| Feedback | Gradually suppresses, opponent starts to struggle | Defends with full strength, looks for flaws |
| Game | Head-on crushing | Skirmishing, luring the enemy, using the environment to pin them down |

```
❌ The two exchanged a palm strike and each retreated three steps.

✅ The palms collided, and a shockwave exploded.
   He took five steps back to steady his posture; the opponent only took two.
   The superior and inferior were immediately clear—but he still had a backup plan.
   The jade slip in his sleeve had already been quietly gripped in his palm.
```

### 3. Same Realm, Nearly Equal

> Applicable: Cultivation of the two is almost identical. Pure contest of spells, tactics, willpower, and magical treasures.

Fighting style refers to the three major tactics of universal [Game] (exploiting flaws/feigning flaws/leveraging momentum), with Xianxia adding probing depths, magical treasure counters, and trapping enemies with formations.


### 4. Combat Manifestation Divided by Cultivation Stage

Cultivators at different stages have completely different combat "styles":

| Stage | Spiritual Energy | Perception | Attack Method | Feedback Magnitude | Representative Writing |
|------|------|------|---------|---------|---------|
| **Qi Condensation** | Thin spiritual energy, struggles to throw a fireball | Naked eye + basic perception | Basic spells, talismans, close-quarters swordsmanship, fists and feet | Blowing a dirt pit, scorching a patch of ground | "He formed a seal, a fireball sparked from his fingertips, and he threw it to explode into a shower of sparks." |
| **Foundation Establishment** | Spiritual energy takes shape, can control objects | Externalizing spiritual energy for perception | Basic flying sword control, enhanced spells, accelerated movement | Sword qi chopping off tree branches, smashing walls | "With a flash of sword light, an arm-thick branch three zhang away was cleanly severed at the root." |
| **Golden Core** | Solid spiritual energy, Golden Core as the core | Divine sense newly opened (within ten zhang) | True power of magical treasures, divine sense pressure, Golden Core phenomena | Flattening a small hilltop, divine sense shattering the sea of consciousness | "The Golden Core trembled, pressure poured out like a tide, and the Qi Condensation cultivator opposite directly fell to his knees." |
| **Nascent Soul** | Spiritual energy forms an infant, nearly limitless | Divine sense covers a hundred zhang | Nascent soul leaves the body, dharma idol newly appears, rudimentary domain | Flattening half a mountain, changing local weather | "The Nascent Soul left the body, a phantom condensed between heaven and earth, and with one palm strike, a hundred-zhang radius was turned to scorched earth." |
| **Soul Formation** | Spiritual energy connects with heaven and earth | Divine sense covers a hundred miles | Law of Heaven and Earth, words become laws, utilizing the power of heaven and earth | Mountains and rivers shatter, astronomical phenomena mutate | "A Soul Formation cultivator enraged, and the clouds within a thousand miles were instantly emptied out." |
| **Tribulation / Mahayana** | Approaching ascension, nearly an immortal | Divine sense covers a thousand miles | Law manipulation, power of heavenly tribulation, spatial tearing | Void rifts, twisting of rules | "When Mahayana cultivators fight, it is not an exchange of spells, but a collision of the rules of heaven and earth—one side wants fire not to burn, the other wants water not to flow." |

### Spells / Spiritual Energy

| ❌ Forbidden | ✅ Mandatory |
|---------|--------|
| Fire, shoot out | Activate, detonate, condense, explode, infuse, amplify |

### Body Cultivation / Physical Body

Same as the fierce type (bombard, cleave, smash, shatter) in the universal [Verb] table, but adding spiritual energy infusion details.

```
❌ He summoned his flying sword and stabbed at the opponent.

✅ Guiding a sword seal with his fingertips, the long sword on his back rang out as it unsheathed, transforming into a stream of light that pierced straight into the clouds,
   then turned mid-air, a point of cold light condensing at the sword tip, thrusting straight down like a falling meteor.
```

**Forbidden:** Using modern cold weapon actions to write Xianxia combat (e.g., "he hacked over with a blade").


## [Feedback] Xianxia Version of Power Feedback

The formula from universal [Feedback] remains unchanged, but the feedback material is replaced with the Xianxia version:

### Universal Power Formula (Xianxia Version)

```
Attack action + Spiritual energy eruption + Heavenly anomaly + Aftermath/Aftershock
```

### Three Sets of Feedback Templates

```
Heaven and Earth Feedback: Spiritual energy oscillating, winds and clouds changing color, earth cracking, sky exploding with sound
Human Body Feedback: Qi and blood surging, agonizing pain in meridians, protective spiritual light shattering, figure retreating violently
Aftermath Feedback: Sword qi aftermath flattening a hilltop, palm wind sweeping past and snapping all trees
```

```
❌ He launched a palm strike with immense power.

✅ Pushing out a single palm, the spiritual energy within a hundred-zhang radius was suddenly emptied out.
   A transparent ripple exploded between heaven and earth; wherever it passed, rocks shattered and trees snapped.
   The hilltop opposite was swept by the palm wind's aftermath, and half the mountainside slid down silently.
```

## [Pacing] Xianxia Pacing Differences

The four-shot switching method from universal [Pacing] is retained, but the pacing distribution is different:

### Xianxia Combat Pacing Characteristics

| Stage | Universal Pacing | Xianxia Pacing |
|------|---------|---------|
| Standoff | Long sentences, eyes, aura | Long sentences, collision of spiritual energy fields, divine sense probing, mutually detecting depths |
| Clash | Barrage of short sentences | Medium speed—spells require chanting / hand seals / sword seal guidance, not instant attacks |
| Turning Point | Injury / power reversal | Trump card summoned (magical treasure/secret art/breakthrough), temporary realm eruption |
| Ending | Display of cost | Opponent flees / spiritual energy depleted / foundation damaged—Xianxia rarely ends in physical death by beating |

```
Long Shot: A thousand miles away, spiritual energy boiled like boiling water, and the clouds on the horizon were dyed dark red.
Medium Shot: Two sword lights chased and collided in the sea of clouds, every impact exploding a ring of shockwaves.
Close-Up: Cold sweat slid down his temple, his fingertips constantly forming seals, and blood had already begun to seep from the corner of his mouth.
POV: The opponent's sword intent rushed at his face; locked onto by that sword in his sea of consciousness, there was no way to evade.
```


## [Game] Xianxia Tactics Expansion

The three major tactics from universal [Game] are retained, adding Xianxia-specific tactics:

### Xianxia Specific Tactics

| Tactic | Description | Effect |
|------|------|------|
| **Probing Depths** | First use minor spells to test the depth of the opponent's cultivation, then decide what level of trump card to use | Avoid blindly using ultimate moves |
| **Magical Treasure Counters** | Mutual generation and restriction of the Five Elements, attribute counters—fire attribute treasures counter wood attribute spells | Not a pure contest of power |
| **Formation Trapping** | Laying out a formation while fighting, turning the battlefield into one's home ground | Classic method of the weak defeating the strong |
| **Feigning Weakness** | Intentionally getting hit to make the opponent think victory is assured, then counter-killing with a magical treasure | Reversal satisfaction |


## [Consequence] Xianxia Cost

Universal [Consequence] is retained, with Xianxia-specific cost types:

| Cost Type | How to Write |
|---------|------|
| **Damaged Foundation** | Meridians shattered into inches, cracks appearing in the Dantian, cultivation dropping in realm |
| **Damaged Magical Treasure** | Cracks appearing on the intrinsic magical treasure, artifact spirit falling into slumber, drop in the treasure's grade |
| **Burning Lifespan** | Exchanging a century of lifespan for temporary power |
| **Cultivation Deviation** | Spiritual energy losing control, inner demons breeding, temporary loss of sanity |

```
❌ He was severely injured.

✅ He knelt on one knee, agonizing pain like shattering coming from his Dantian—a fine crack had appeared on the surface of his Golden Core.
   He reached out and wiped the blood from the corner of his mouth, smiled, and summoned that jade slip he shouldn't have used.
```
