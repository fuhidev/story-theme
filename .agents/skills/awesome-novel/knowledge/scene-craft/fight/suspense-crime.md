# Combat Scene Writing: Suspense & Crime

> Overwrites parts of the universal combat methodology (`fight/universal.md`) that are not suitable for the suspense and crime genre.

- Confrontation is not a test of martial prowess, but a pursuit of information. Most "battles" are pursuits, tailing, searches, and psychological games in closed rooms.
- There is no concept of "combat power"; the outcome of a confrontation is determined by information gaps and on-site conditions.
- Visualization of cost: The mental cost during the investigation process > physical cost—insomnia and psychological pressure after being threatened are more impactful than superficial flesh wounds.
- **Pitfall Avoidance**: Do not write detectives engaging in fistfights—the core of suspense/crime is intellectual confrontation, not physical combat.
