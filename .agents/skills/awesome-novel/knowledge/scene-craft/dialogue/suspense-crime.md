# Dialogue Scene Writing: Suspense & Crime

> Overwrites parts of the universal dialogue methodology (`dialogue/universal.md`) that are not suitable for the suspense and crime genre.

- Dialogue is an information war. Every line carries information (clues/misdirection/probing), with no meaningless social pleasantries.
- "Characters not telling the truth" in suspense is not determined by personality, but by the case itself—what each character hides is a clue in itself.
- Power dynamics: The unequal power between the interrogator and the interrogated persists throughout. Detectives use short sentences + silence to apply pressure, while suspects use rhetorical questions + evasion to defend.
- **Pitfall Avoidance**: Do not write dialogues where citizens explain the case to the police at length ("Here's what happened, that night..."). Information should be revealed through investigation and physical evidence, not by characters talking.
