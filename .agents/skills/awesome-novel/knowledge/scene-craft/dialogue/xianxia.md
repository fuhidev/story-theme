# Dialogue Scene Writing: Eastern Xianxia

> Overwrites parts of the universal dialogue methodology (`dialogue/universal.md`) that are not suitable for the Xianxia genre.

- Seniority and cultivation realm determine speaking rights. Juniors cannot look seniors in the eye, cannot interrupt, and must use honorifics; seniors can be condescending to juniors but must also maintain an air of dignity.
- "Characters not telling the truth" in Xianxia manifests as "hidden meanings in words"—when seniors give guidance, they don't give answers directly, but speak in riddles to test comprehension; villains speak arrogantly but hide their trump cards.
- Realm suppression directly maps to conversational suppression—those with high realms can speak casually without fear of giving offense; those with low realms weigh every word carefully.
- Actions in Xianxia dialogue are not holding a coffee cup, but flicking sleeves, cupping hands in greeting, pouring tea, forming hand seals, and releasing spiritual pressure.
- **Pitfall Avoidance**: Do not write modern colloquial style Xianxia dialogue ("Are you okay?" "I'm fine"), and do not write characters explaining the cultivation system at length.
