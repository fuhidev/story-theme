# Book Naming Methodology

> A reader's attention span for a new book is only one or two seconds. In that one second, they can't see your writing style; they only see your book title.
> The following three techniques are summarized from the experience of blockbuster web novel titles.

---

## 1. Creating Extreme Contrast — Triggering Curiosity

**Core:** Create cognitive conflict. If a reader has a "?" pop up in their mind after reading, the first step is successful.

### Contrast Formula

```
Identity (Bottom-tier/Ordinary) + Behavior (Earth-shattering/Unattainable)
```

```
❌ Plain and straightforward: The Strongest Soldier King in the City

✅ Extreme contrast: Discharged Soldier King Starts by Being Dumped by a Beautiful CEO, Turns Around and Marries Her Mother
   → Strong/weak contrast + Identity dislocation + Ethical conflict

✅ High contrast: System Gives a Civil Service Job, From Deliveryman to 007 Agent
   → The huge gap between a deliveryman and 007 is the driving force for readers to click.
```

**Key Point:** The book title is not meant to summarize the entire text, but to create suspense.

---

## 2. High-Density Tag Stacking — Lowering Filtering Costs

**Core:** Modern readers are lazy; they just want to know what genre you are writing, what cheat the protagonist has, and what the satisfying points are. Slap the core selling points right in the reader's face.

### Universal Formula

```
Genre Background + Core Cheat + Extreme Satisfying Goal
```

```
❌ Doomsday Survival Record (Too broad, no informational value)

✅ Global Deep Freeze: I Hoard Tens of Billions in Supplies with Infinite Space
   → Global Deep Freeze (Background) + Infinite Space (Cheat) + Hoard Tens of Billions in Supplies (Satisfying point)

✅ National Destiny Survival, I Feed the Country with an Intelligence System
   → National Destiny Survival (Background) + Intelligence System (Cheat) + Feed the Country (Satisfying point)
```

**Key Point:** A long name means more tags, and more tags mean a broader audience. On recommendation-driven platforms, precise tags are the key to traffic.

---

## 3. Precise Emotional Grasping — Hitting the Reader's Heart

**Core:** A good book title stirs up the reader's emotions before the story even begins.

### Two Types of Emotions

| Type | Mechanism | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Explosion after Frustration** | Low point setting + Climax result, the thrill of face-slapping | "After Divorce, the Aloof Female CEO Begs Me on Her Knees to Get Back Together" — Divorce (Frustration) + Begging on knees (Explosion) |
| **Extreme Superiority** | Letting the reader step into the protagonist's overpowered life | Identity dislocation + Crushing face-slapping |

### Verbs with Built-In Emotional Energy

```
Beg on knees, backhand, shock, whole family regrets, crying and begging, instantly dumbfounded, instant explosion
```

**Key Point:** These words come with emotional energy, making readers feel the urge that "I won't be able to sleep tonight if I don't click in to take a look."

---

## Tips for Testing Book Titles

Prepare three names, following the contrast route, the tag route, and the emotion route respectively. Go to a reader group or your social circle and ask: "If it were you, which of these three names would you most want to click on?"

Do not work behind closed doors, and do not fall into self-indulgence. **The book title is written for the readers to see, not for yourself.**
